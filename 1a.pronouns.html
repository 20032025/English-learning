GRAMMAR

Subject Pronouns

ضمائر الفاعل

01

أنا

ay

I am a student. أنا تلميذ / تلميذة.
02

You

أنتَ / أنتِ

yoo

You are my friend. أنت صديقي / صديقتي.
03

He

هو

hee

He is a teacher. هو أستاذ.
04

She

هي

shee

She is a doctor. هي طبيبة.
05

It

هو / هي لغير العاقل

it

It is a cat. إنها قطة.
06

We

نحن

wee

We are happy. نحن سعداء.
07

You

أنتم / أنتن

yoo

You are students. أنتم تلاميذ.
08

They

هم / هن

thay

They are friends. هم أصدقاء.

TIPS

Important Tips

ملاحظات مهمة

I is always written with a capital letter.

He is used for a male person.

She is used for a female person.

It is used for things and animals when we do not specify gender.

You can mean one person or more than one person.

They is used for more than one person or thing.
